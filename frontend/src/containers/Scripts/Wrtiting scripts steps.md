# Writing Scripts Steps

## Step 1: Write a script prompt

- Write 3-5 sentences about the topic you want to make a video about.
- Address the subject
- Don’t over-prescribe
- Don’t include links

## Step 2:

- Research:
  - The AI will do this for you and create a 360 degree view of the topic
  - It provides:
    - Executive Summary:
    - Key context:
    - Key facts (5 to 10) each one with a shock score /10
    - Interesting stats and findings (Top 5) each one with a shock score /10
    - Common misconceptions vs reality (3 to 5) each one with a shock score /10
    - Analogies and simple comparisons
    - How it works (ELI5)
    - Real world use cases today
    - Major trends
    - Sources

## Step 3: Pick an angle

- The AI will provide top topic angles for your script from the research done in step 2.
- You can pick (one or multiple) angles from the 5 angles ai generated, or from favorite angles templates list.

## Step 4: Pick favorite hooks

- The AI will provide up to 5 hooks for your script based on the angle picked in step 3.
- You can pick (one or multiple) hooks from the 5 hooks ai generated, or from favorite hooks templates list.
- when you choose a hooks template, the AI will generate an adapted hook based on that hook template.

## Step 5: Pick favorit style

- The AI will provide 5 styles for your script based on the angle picked in step 3.
- You can pick (one or multiple) styles from the 5 styles ai generated, or from favorite styles templates list.
- when you choose a style template, the AI will generate an adapted style based on that style template.

## Step 6: Generate scripts

- The AI will generate scripts based on hooks and angles picked in step 4, and styles picked in step 5.
- You can edit the scripts as you wish.
- You can ask AI to refine the scripts as you wish.
